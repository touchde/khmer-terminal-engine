# Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, manage releases, and make final decisions. Current maintainer: Den TOUCH (`touchde` on GitHub).
- **Spec reviewers** are native Khmer readers and terminal developers who review changes to the KTE Width Spec.
- **Contributors**: anyone who opens an issue or pull request.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day changes: lazy consensus. A PR merges after maintainer approval and no unresolved objections.
- Significant technical decisions are recorded as Architecture Decision Records in [`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/).
- Spec changes need approval from a maintainer **and** a native Khmer reader (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of quality contributions and reviews may be invited by existing maintainers.
